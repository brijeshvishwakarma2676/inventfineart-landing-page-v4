## 2. Two things we will not do

1. **Use internet photos with watermarks removed** (L286, L366). Removing a watermark from someone else's photo is copyright infringement. A live business site can get takedown notices or legal claims, and Google can delist it.
   - **Instead:** his own photos first, as he himself prefers (L368–372). For gaps (e.g. wall features): royalty-free photos whose licence allows commercial use (Unsplash, Pexels), unedited and credited where required, or a line drawing until his own photos arrive.
2. **Copy competitor text** (yogigrc.com). We write original text from his facts (1.5). This avoids copyright problems and Google's duplicate-content penalty.

## 3. Plan

### Phase A: Structure (no new photos needed; current photos stand in until his arrive)
1. **Services: from 5 lines to 4**
   - New data in `app/data/lines.js`: Sculptures, Murals & wall features, Planters, and Customised designer products in FRP & GRC. Each has segments (1.3) and a write-up from his words.
   - Pages: `/sculptures`, `/murals-wall-features`, `/planters`, `/customised-products` (final names to confirm, see §5).
   - **301 redirects:** `/designer-planters` → `/planters`, and `/grc-products`, `/grc-grilles`, `/frp-products` → `/customised-products`. Old v2 and .html links follow on.
   - **Design codes never change** (IFA-SC-…, IFA-PL-…, IFA-GRC-…, IFA-GRL-…, IFA-FRP-…). GRC, GRL and FRP designs move under Customised products with their codes unchanged. New photos get new codes.
   - **Segments on each service page:** tabs or rows per segment (e.g. Planters: FRP · GRC · Removable · Partition creeper · Tray flower beds), each with its photos. An empty segment shows "Photos coming soon" with Enquire, never fake photos.
2. **Home order** (1.2):
   - Hero → 4 services (4 photos each) → calibre band → "Few executed works" (4–5 photos plus a Gallery button) → materials (GRC | FRP with photos, samples and advantages) → why choose us → contact.
   - The process, clients and FAQ sections stay between "Few executed works" and "Why choose us" unless you want them removed (see §5).
3. **Rename "Projects" to "Gallery"** in the menu, the page and the links; `/projects` 301s to `/gallery`. Gallery groups: Murals & relief art · Water features & rockery · Pergolas & gazebos · plus the new services.
4. **Get a Quote:**
   - **Remove** the oxide "Get a Quote" block from the header and the hero buttons.
   - **Keep:** floating WhatsApp, the phone number in the header, and the enquiry or contact section **at the end** of pages.
   - The enquiry form itself stays, because it emails him.
5. **Why choose us:** a new section near the end of Home. The text is built from his own statements in the meeting and on the old site (factory open 24×7 is his own words, L410; made to design; start-to-end solutions; turnkey projects; quality checked; packing and fixing).
6. **Details on click:** add `location`, `client` and `material` fields to designs and projects. The lightbox caption and card show them when filled; empty fields are hidden. He supplies these with the photos.
7. **Materials section** (§1.5, §1.10 c–f):
   - GRC and FRP side by side, each with three product groups: **Jaali · Pots & planters · Building products**.
   - A short line for the other materials (stainless steel, stone, bronze…) so every material is covered.
   - GRC: just two blocks, **Advantages** and **Why GRC**.
   - Under each: 3–4 photos, colour samples (placeholders until he sends them) and an **advantages** list.
   - **FRP:** from his words (1.5).
   - **GRC:** our own wording of standard GRC benefits (lightweight compared with precast concrete, mouldable into fine detail, durable outdoors). This is **marked PROVISIONAL for him to confirm**, because the claims-check rules forbid unconfirmed specs.
8. **Brochure:** not shown at launch (1.6). If he sends one: an in-page viewer of page images, with no PDF file published, so there is nothing to download (screenshots remain possible, as he accepted at L482).
9. **Hero:** keep the drawing hero. In Phase B, redraw the scenes from his photos.
